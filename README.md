4.2 Data Sensitivity
Data sensitivity describes how confidential or important the information involved in an incident is.
For example, public company information is generally less sensitive than customer identity records or internal financial information.
What I expect the dashboard to show under different conditions
Classification	Sensitivity	Meaning
Restricted	Highest	Highly sensitive information requiring the strongest protection, such as financial account details or authentication credentials.
Confidential	High	Private business information, such as internal reports, nonpublic employee information, or customer records.
Internal	Low–Moderate	Routine internal information, such as operating instructions or workflow documentation.
Public	Lowest	Information intended for public access, such as published reports or announcements.
Unclassified	Unknown	Information that has not yet been assigned a sensitivity level. Its actual sensitivity is unknown.
